# Draft content and marks terms

Status: proposal for legal review under CODEX-DG. These terms are not effective
and do not replace the repository's current MIT license.

## Documentation and tutorial content

Proposed license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (`CC-BY-4.0`).

The scope would include prose documentation and tutorials expressly marked as
covered. Source code embedded in documentation remains under the license identified
for that snippet or its containing software package, not automatically CC-BY.

Attribution would be satisfied by retaining the OpenWebHMI name, copyright notice,
license link, and a link to the source document where reasonably practicable.

## Screenshots and demo media

Proposed default: `CC-BY-4.0` for OpenWebHMI-owned screenshots, diagrams, and
marketing/demo media expressly marked as covered. Asset-specific notices override
the default. Customer material, vendor artwork, third-party marks, confidential
plant information, and assets without verified provenance are not eligible.

Executable demo code, configuration logic, and scenario code remain software and
follow the AGPL/MPL path matrix rather than this content license.

## OpenWebHMI name and marks

OpenWebHMI's name and logos are currently unregistered marks. Registration is not
a prerequisite to publishing a trademark policy or claiming the marks. The
project may use `TM` where useful, but must not use `®` or state that a mark is
registered unless and until registration covering the relevant goods/services is
actually granted.

Proposed policy:

- permit truthful nominative use when referring to the official project;
- permit unmodified redistribution to identify the software accurately;
- permit statements of compatibility such as “works with OpenWebHMI” when they
  do not imply endorsement;
- require modified distributions and forks to use a distinct product name and
  branding unless written permission is granted;
- prohibit confusing use, false endorsement, or presentation as an official
  release, partner, certification, or support channel;
- allow editorial, educational, and community discussion uses consistent with
  applicable trademark law.

The final policy must identify Sergio Gallegos as the present owner, define
official marks/assets, provide a permission contact, and state plainly that the
marks are unregistered. Copyright licensing of a logo and permission to use a
trademark are separate questions.

## User content

Proposed policy intent: OpenWebHMI does not claim ownership of plant projects,
tag databases, historian/process data, PLC programs, drawings, graphics, scripts,
or other content created or supplied by an operator. The final terms must distinguish
user content from modifications or derivative works of AGPL software and must not
promise that every plugin or linked module falls outside AGPL without legal review.
